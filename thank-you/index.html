What happens next

  1. Receipt. Paddle (our merchant of record) emails your receipt and invoice right away.
  2. License key. Keys are issued by hand — your key will be emailed to your checkout address, usually within 24 hours.
  3. Activate. In LogLens: Settings → License tab (设置 → 许可页签) → paste the key → Activate. Works fully offline.

Nothing arrived after 24 hours? Check spam first, then reach us — we will make it right.
