LogLens丹青日志

The fast viewer for huge log and JSONL files on Windows. Memory-mapped engine: opens 1 GB logs in about a second, searches them in ~100 ms. Free tier forever.

Why LogLens

  • Gigabyte-scale in milliseconds — memory-mapped, line-indexed; random access at 0.5 µs/line. 10 GB files open in seconds.
  • JSONL as a table — columns auto-detected, nested rows expand in place, field filters without writing a query language.
  • Multi-file timeline merge — merge logs from several services by timestamp and trace one request across them. (v1.x)
  • Field analytics — p50/p99 percentiles and value distributions on any JSON field. (v1.x)
  • Export — filtered results as raw lines, pretty JSON, or Excel-ready CSV. (v1.x)
  • Named workspace sessions — save and restore your whole triage setup: filters, columns, expansions. (v1.x)
  • Chinese-friendly — GBK / UTF-16 / UTF-8 detection that just works; Chinese text searchable.
  • Portable — a single <5 MB zip, no installer, no registry writes.
